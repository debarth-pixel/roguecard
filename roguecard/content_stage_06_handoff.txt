Content Stage 06 Handoff: Shop Heal Service

Completion Status
- Completed and verified on 2026-04-03.

Objective
- Add a second shop-only credit sink with immediate player value.
- Reduce excess end-of-run credits without destabilizing the current reward, shop, event, save, or QoL systems.

What Shipped
- Added a repeatable shop heal service directly into the existing shop inventory flow.
- Chosen heal model:
  - flat `14 HP` for `18 credits`
  - reasoning: a flat service is easy to read, easy to value at a glance, and easy to keep consistent in UI, save state, and economy simulation.
- The heal service:
  - appears as a normal shop offer alongside card offers, reroll, and purge
  - is usable only while the player is below max HP
  - stays repeatable within the same shop visit
  - clears selection cleanly after purchase
  - blocks wasteful or invalid purchases with readable reasons
- Older `v5` active-shop saves restore safely:
  - missing reroll keys still default correctly
  - missing heal service gets inserted back into old active shops without a save-format bump

Audit Findings
- The heal sink does add real spend pressure and modestly improves run survivability, but it is not strong enough by itself to fix the excess-currency problem.
- On 200 seeds, the heal-enabled stage averaged:
  - `57.71` final credits
  - `1.03` paid sinks
  - `0.29` heal-service uses
  - `0.94` win rate
  - `37.92` final HP
  - `11.62` final deck size
- Compared with the reroll-only Stage 05 economy:
  - final credits improved by `0.56`
  - paid sinks improved by `0.18`
  - heal uses improved by `0.29`
  - win rate stayed flat
  - final HP improved by `3.29`
- Result:
  - the economy is still not healthy
  - a third shop-only sink is still recommended

- 200-seed economy audit via `simulate_run_economy(seed_count=200, start_seed=1)`.

Known Remaining Issues
- End-of-run credits are still too high on average.
- The heal sink mostly helps injured runs; it does not capture enough excess currency from healthier runs.
- Shops still need one more compelling spend option for players who are healthy and already satisfied with the current card row.

Recommendation
- The next safe balancing step is a third shop-only sink aimed at healthy runs.
- Best fit:
  - a small permanent max-HP service or similarly immediate non-deck-bloating purchase
- Reason:
  - heal is only attractive when injured, so healthy runs still exit shops with too much currency.

Combat and map clarity pass for the main gameplay loop.

Audit findings
- Combat needed stronger previews for costs, targets, and invalid actions.
- The map needed to make currently reachable routes more obvious and stop stale hover/focus states from obscuring the next legal path.
- Returning from combat needed to preserve route clarity without introducing a reward layer that does not exist yet.

Player-facing changes shipped
- Combat now previews projected damage, block, heal, draw, and energy outcomes directly in the card-inspection panel.
- Damage cards now prefer a hovered enemy target when available, improving targeting clarity without changing the underlying combat rules.
- Enemy-turn input now denies gracefully with readable feedback instead of feeling unresponsive.
- Map focus now prefers currently available routes, highlights reachable paths more strongly, labels the current position more clearly, and shows a direct “next action” hint.
- Available routes now sync mouse hover and keyboard selection so confirm actions are predictable.
